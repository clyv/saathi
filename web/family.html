Who is Saathi for?

Family members

Saathi uses this to talk about the people they love, and to remind them to call. Write names the way they are said, in Hindi if you can (e.g. क्लिविन rather than Clivin), so the voice pronounces them right.

Safety

If they mention chest pain, a fall, trouble breathing or a scam call, the screen shows these numbers in large text.

Saathi's face and voice

Advanced

Messages from family

Leave a note and Saathi will pass it on, in its own words, at the start of the next call. For example: “I'll video call you on Sunday at 7.”

    What Saathi remembers

    After calls, Saathi notes a few facts to bring up later (“she made gajar halwa on Sunday”). They stay on this computer. Delete anything you don't want kept.
